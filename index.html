// about

Studying the systems attackers exploit — and building the ones that catch them.

Sonu is a B.Tech Computer Science student specializing in Cybersecurity at RTC Institute of Technology, under Jharkhand University of Technology (JUT), Ranchi.

Outside coursework, Sonu builds detection tools, competes in tech fests, and runs CyyberTalks — a cybersecurity content channel that covers breaches and threat intelligence with an India-relevant lens. Sonu also interned at a Cyber Crime Police Station in Ranchi, and is active as an NCC Cadet and member of IIC and IETE.

Long-term focus: a career in government cybersecurity — DRDO Scientist B, CERT-In, or NIC.
